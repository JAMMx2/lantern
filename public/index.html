✦ Lantern

Free AI that works for you. Pick one way to switch it on: there's no account with us and nothing to pay.

1 / Get a free AI key. About 2 minutes, no credit card.
    Openopens in a new tab
    Also recognises keys from OpenRouter, NVIDIA, Hugging Face, SambaNova, Mistral, DeepSeek, OpenAI, xAI, Together and Fireworks. Each key only goes to the service it belongs to. Free services have their own privacy terms; for anything private, use AI on this computer.
    2 / Or use AI already on this computer. No terminal needed.
    3 / Your AI. Check it, then save.
    Lantern uses these top to bottom. When one is busy it moves to the next, so a second free key means you're never stuck. Paid services always go last.
    4 / Take it to another device with a Lantern Pass.
    Paste a pass into Spark on another computer, or open the link in any browser to set up Lantern Lite in one step. Made from your saved chain. The passphrase is never inside the pass or the link: keep it, or send it separately.
